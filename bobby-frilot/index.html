About Bobby

Building websites that work as hard as the businesses behind them.

Bobby Frilot III founded SNRG WEB to provide small businesses with a direct, practical alternative to bloated website projects and generic templates. His work combines front-end development, mobile-first design, technical SEO and conversion strategy into websites built to be useful, fast and easy to maintain.

Through SNRG WEB, Bobby works across the full website lifecycle—from structure and design through schema markup, local SEO, analytics, hosting and ongoing support. His professional background also includes commercial parking operations and business development, where the same focus on execution, presentation, customer experience and measurable growth carries into his work.

Born and raised in New Orleans, Bobby and his family have made Nashville home. He is married and a father of four, and SNRG WEB reflects the practical, entrepreneurial approach he brings to both business and family life.
